Pick Your Path

Seven battle-tested roads from "Hello World" to hired. Each one lists every skill you need — in the order you need it. Start anywhere, switch anytime.

Friendly start

Frontend Developer

Build everything the user sees and touches. The most visual path — you get instant results on screen, which makes it the classic first choice for students.

HTMLCSSJavaScriptTypeScriptReactTailwind CSSGit

Roughly 6–9 months to first internship-ready projects

Open Roadmap
Moderate

Backend Developer

Own the logic, databases and APIs behind the interface. Huge demand from Tunisia's outsourcing and product companies — most internship offers here are backend or full-stack.

Node.jsPythonSQLREST APIsAuthDockerCaching

Roughly 8–12 months to internship-ready

Open Roadmap
Advanced

AI & Data Science

Turn data into predictions and products. The hottest field on campus — Tunisia has real players (think Instadeep's story) — but expect heavy math and often a master's degree.

PythonLinear AlgebraStatisticsPandasscikit-learnTensorFlowSQL

Roughly 12–18 months — strongest with a master's

Open Roadmap
Advanced

Cybersecurity

Learn how systems break — and how to defend them. Banks, telecoms and SOC teams in Tunis are hiring, and the CTF community (Securinets & friends) is a brilliant training ground.

LinuxNetworkingPythonCryptographyBurp SuiteSIEMCTFs

12+ months — fundamentals first, tools second

Open Roadmap
Moderate

DevOps & Cloud

Automate everything between "code written" and "code running in production". Fewer juniors compete here, salaries are strong, and it's one of the most remote-friendly paths for Tunisian engineers.

LinuxDockerKubernetesCI/CDAWSTerraformMonitoring

Roughly 9–12 months — best after some dev experience

Open Roadmap
Moderate

Mobile Development

Put real apps in people's pockets. Flutter lets you ship to Android and iOS from one codebase — a favorite for Tunisian freelance gigs and startup MVPs.

DartFlutterKotlinSwiftReact NativeFirebase

Roughly 6–10 months to a first published app

Open Roadmap
Advanced

Game Development

Engine, physics, art pipelines — the most demanding but most fun you'll have debugging. The local industry is small, so aim for remote studios or go indie and ship on Steam.

C#UnityC++UnrealBlenderShadersGame Design

12+ months — ship small games early, not one dream game

Open Roadmap

Still torn between two?

Here's the honest student trick: pick the one you'd build a small project in this weekend. Motivation compounds faster than any "optimal" choice — and you can always pivot in your first two years.
